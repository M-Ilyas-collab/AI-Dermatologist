#  AI Dermatologist :
A web app that helps users analyze skin conditions by uploading an image.
# Problem Solved :
Many people in my community don’t have easy access to dermatologists. This app provides quick, AI-powered preliminary analysis to guide them toward   professional care.

 ## AI Feature
**What it does:**  
The AI system powers multiple features inside the app, making it more than just a photo analyzer.  

 **Skin Scan & Photo Upload** 
 Users can either scan their skin live or upload an image for AI analysis.  
**Detailed Instructions** 
After analysis, the app provides clear guidance about the detected skin damage or condition.  
 **Interactive AI Chatbot**  
 Users can chat with the AI to ask questions and explore general or major skin issues for free.  
 **History Tracking**
 Every scan result is saved in the user’s history, so they can revisit past analyses later.  
 **User Accounts & Profiles**
 Users can sign up, create a profile, and manage their saved results.  
 **Appointment Booking** 
 The app allows users to book an appointment with a dermatologist for further consultation.  
 **Mobile App Download** 
 On phones, users can download the full app for themselves, ensuring easy access on the go.
